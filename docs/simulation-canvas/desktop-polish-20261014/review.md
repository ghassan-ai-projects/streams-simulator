## Findings and dispositions

**UX-01 — verification label ambiguity — resolved.** On a full reload, all nine rows begin at `Not checked`. Opening `w-evaluate` displays `Verified`; returning to Workspace updates that row to `Verified`. Session-cached verdicts are distinguished from unchecked runs. The fixture's edited and missing-digest records show `Not verified` in the list after opening them.

**UX-02 — incomplete fault-plan readiness — resolved.** On Campus run setup, adding a fault changes the summary to `1 fault needs attention before starting.` With `h02/M-1` selected and fault kind empty, Start focuses the missing kind field and reports `Fault 1 needs a fault.` plus `1 to fix before starting: Fault 1 needs a fault.` No recording is created.

No other actionable visual defect is confirmed. At the factory graph's 50% initial zoom, the edge of the graph extends outside 1280 and 1440 viewports, preserving larger node values and labels. At 1280, `Fit the plant in view` reduces to 29% and brings every graph node into view in both themes; titles become abbreviated, with selection and the inspector available for detail. The 1920 view fits at 52%. This is a deliberate detail-versus-overview tradeoff; the page shell itself does not overflow.
