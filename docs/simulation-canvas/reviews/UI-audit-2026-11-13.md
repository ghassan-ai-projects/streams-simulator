## What was wrong

| # | Screen | Finding | Expectation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plant | The conveyor has 35 links between 5 instances. Every link was its own wire from an output pin on the right to an input pin on the left, so the 21 links from a drive back to the belt each looped around the nodes. Nothing could be followed. | P-4, P-14 |
| 2 | Plant | Each node was a list of model ports (share, stop ok, run cap, boost cap, ...). The measured channels an engineer looks for (current, winding, vibration) were only in the inspector. | P-2, P-7 |
| 3 | Review | A node listed one row per port. Most conveyor ports carry no measured channel, so a drive showed 13 rows of "·" and its five readings were behind "+5 more in Inspect". The plant fitted at 47 %. | V-4, V-14 |
| 4 | Plant | The feeder was drawn after the belt it feeds, because the belt's id sorts first. | P-3 |
| 5 | Plant | `bulk_flow` and `share_weight` had no port family: the feed was an untyped grey wire. | P-4 |
| 6 | Plant | The legend listed all nine port families, seven of which this plant does not use. | P-11 |
| 7 | Workspace | The "Did not finish" chip had no padding, radius or gap: its icon touched the text. | W-11 |
| 8 | Tests | `shipped-units` failed on main: the outcome unit "equivalent production hours" had no presentation mapping. | G-3 |

## What changed

| # | Change | Commit |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 | **Group links.** A plant where two instances share three or more links one way opens with one wire per pair of instances. The wire carries the number of links, an arrow each way, and leaves from the sides of the two nodes that face each other. Nodes list their measured channels with units on the plant screen and their readings in a review, up to six. "Group links" in the graph toolbar switches back to one wire per link, and is offered wherever two instances share more than one link. The pair's direction, and so the layout order, follows the way that carries more material. `bulk_flow` has its own family and colour; `share_weight` is a signal. | c3b0ba41 |
| 5 | A grouped wire takes the colour of the one material or power family it carries, and is grey when it carries several. | 0d375a16 |
| 6 | The legend lists only the families the plant uses, explains the count on a grouped wire, and leaves out pin states while pins are not drawn. | 699d3032 |
| 7 | The chip has the badge shape. | 7c74ff38 |
| 8 | The unit is presented as written. | dc2b6437 |

Result on the conveyor: 4 wires instead of 35 on the plant screen at 100 %, and a review that fits at 77 % with
every drive's current, winding temperature, vibration, run status and run command readable on its node.

## Known limits, not done here

- Factory utilities still opens with all 59 wires; the legend filter is a tool to thin it, not a new default. A
  cleaner opening view needs wire routing or a layout change.
- From the conveyor review, still open and all needing the binary or the bridge first: drive trips as timeline events,
  an outcome view (cost J against the baselines), loading a scenario file into the run panel, and a visible difference
  between plants A and B beyond the title.
- A review node reserves blank lines for the fault and call notes it will show later in the run; on a node with three
  note lines this is a visible gap until the first event.
- The legend filter is per screen visit: it is not remembered when the plant is opened again.
