## ISS-13D-01 The legend heading overflows its popover on every plant screen
- Severity: Low
- Kind: craft (with a coherence edge)
- Where: The legend popover on the plant view (dir-review-empty, and every plant screen: the legend button sits in the plant action bar), light and dark, 1440 × 900; the same popover on a review for comparison. Screenshot crops of the heading: `41-legend-plant-light-head.png`, `41-legend-plant-dark-head.png`; whole popover `41-legend-plant-light.png` / `41-legend-plant-dark.png` / `26-legend-light.png`, review `42-legend-review-light.png`.
- Observed: the heading "Wire colour and pin shape by port type" needs **212 px** of single-line text in a **194 px** content box (popover 220 px wide, 12 px padding, right edge 1050.6). Because the plant action bar sets `white-space: nowrap`, the whole popover inherits it, so the heading cannot wrap: its text box runs to 1055.3, **4.7 px past the popover's right border** (17.7 px past the content box), and the final "e" of "port type" is drawn on the border in both themes. On the review the same component inherits `white-space: normal`, so the identical heading wraps (184.3 px, ending 9.7 px clear) and the popover is taller (496 px vs 419 px). Round 12 recorded this item as holding — it measured the heading's left inset (13 px, still correct today) and never measured the right side.
- Expected: nothing in a panel overflows its own edge (§3.2 Layout, "nothing overlaps or clips"; §3.2 Craft/Consistency, the same component renders the same on every screen).
- Suggestion: reset `white-space: normal` (or shorten the copy to fit 194 px) on the legend heading in the legend's own CSS module rather than relying on inherited `nowrap`, and pin it with a component test that asserts the heading's text rect fits its popover's content box at both legend sizes. Takes Craft to 9.
