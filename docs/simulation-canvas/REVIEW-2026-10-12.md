## 1. Owner decisions taken during the review

| Decision | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Drop the agent-involved plant emergency shutdown** | Removes SC §5.2 chain, `t_last_recoverable`, recovery playbooks, run forking ("S4"), human-feasibility checks, the R4 route change in Agentic Stream and the ESD policy contradiction. **Kept:** per-device `safe_stop` (bench), plant hardwired trips and interlocks as the automatic safety layer; reaching a trip is a top-tier safety event |
| **No authentication for now** | Required before any network carrier or customer deployment. **Kept now:** a `simulated`/`hardware` environment marker in `node_birth` (host refuses a mismatch) and a private socket (owner-only permissions) |
| **Defer the "operator takes over" (authority revocation) test** | Removes SC §5.2 revocation event and L5's revocation part, AS §6.1 interlock mapping |

Findings closed by these decisions are not repeated below.

## 4. Findings — plant-wire protocol (PW)

| # | Sev | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | High | §3 contradicts §14: `device_birth` still lists full metrics/commands; `node_birth` lacks contract version/digest and `protocol_version` | Rewrite §3 rows: `node_birth` = kinds, contract version and digest, environment marker, `bd_seq`; `device_birth` = kind, id, `boot_id`, instance overrides, **current values** |

## 9. What the reviewers judged right

Birth/data/death/rebirth with gap detection; receipt ≠ result ≠ observation ≠ verification;
supervisory layer non-real-time with firmware limits and hardwired trips working without the
link; gateway as plumbing with dual gating; kind templates with add/narrow overrides; diagnosis
catalog independent of the fault list with decoys; responders as a fixed form; lexicographic
scoring with measured headroom and honest status labels; the real-world-sensor experiment as
acceptance test #1.
