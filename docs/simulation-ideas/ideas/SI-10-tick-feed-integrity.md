## 1. Purpose

A consolidated financial-market data feed across a pool of symbols: torrent event rates, per-symbol activity counters, human-paced manual quotes, heavy-tail cross-venue lateness, duplicates, and late-arriving updates inside and outside a declared delivery allowance. There is no correction event shape: the world never back-dates `event_time`, so "late corrections" are modeled honestly as ordinary updates arriving through a heavy-tailed channel, classified by their ledger lateness.

Exposes at-least-once data-path integrity at volume: inbox dedup that is neither too coarse (collapses distinct updates) nor too fine (double-counts), sequence-gap handling (drop/`delay_tail`-induced), allowance in/out attribution computable from ledger timestamps, and a regulatory-grade halt decision under tempo that reconciles against the human-quoted price anchor. Run by agentic-stream (event_inbox suppression, id-cap scale bounds, watermark-vs-idle) primarily, with tamoz (approval pressure at tempo, exactly-once halt) and streamsim coverage (torrent-shaped G4 conservation evidence the portfolio lacks) as co-beneficiaries.

**Portfolio partition.** Versus SI-02, the interlock story here is halt/ack discipline under torrent tempo with the silent-no-effect lying/telling channel split, not command-journal reconciliation. Versus SI-05, dedup here is at-volume inbox integrity with ledger-anchored counts, not replay mask. Versus SI-08, torrent here stresses correctness at volume and supplies the torrent-shaped G4 conservation evidence the portfolio lacks, while SI-08 keeps consumer backpressure. Residual gap stated plainly: the pairing/join family (identity-pairing across two event sources) remains unhosted this round — no shipped domain exercises it and this domain does not either (`peer_residual` is sibling-deviation, not pairing), so the portfolio sheds nothing there but still lacks the family.

## 2. How it runs on this simulator

Everything below is domain, perturbation and profile **data** through the one schema — zero new machinery, no per-domain code branches. All cited machinery is committed and verified in this tree: human_driven cadence case and exponential human draws ([internal/world/cadence.go:27,83-89](../../../internal/world/cadence.go)), interlock predicate and terminal refusal ([internal/world/effector_policy.go:63-76](../../../internal/world/effector_policy.go); [internal/world/effectors.go:96-105](../../../internal/world/effectors.go) with `ErrInterlockRefused`), assign/additive effect kicks ([internal/world/effects.go:37-52](../../../internal/world/effects.go)), silent-no-effect shadow with mandatory confirmation channels ([internal/world/effectors.go:159-169](../../../internal/world/effectors.go); domain-spec schema [internal/schemas/domain-spec-v0.1.schema.json:513-517,:550-570](../../../internal/schemas/domain-spec-v0.1.schema.json)), lognormal link delay ([internal/world/link_delay.go:31-38](../../../internal/world/link_delay.go)), observation_bias hook (domain-spec schema :256-266), multiplicative fault deltas ([internal/world/state_drivers.go:24-25](../../../internal/world/state_drivers.go)).

### Entities

16 symbol-entities (`sym-{n}`), one entity per symbol, stable pool, no churn. **Venues are two channel families per entity** (`a_*`/`b_*` naming discipline), not entities: the solver has no venue grouping — `peerReadings` walks all entities ([internal/truth/detector_math.go:39-50](../../../internal/truth/detector_math.go)) and `peerResidual` compares against the mean of all siblings ([internal/truth/solver.go:108-115](../../../internal/truth/solver.go)) — so `crossed_venue_divergence` is declared as `channel_divergence` on `(a_mid, b_mid)` of the **same** entity (detector_math.go:16-22), and `peer_residual`, where used, treats the 16 statistically exchangeable symbols (uniform state initials, per-entity noise substreams) as one sibling pool.

### Signals

Per symbol:

- `a_mid` / `b_mid` — scalar, event_driven torrent: resolution crossings driven by declared noise vs resolution at a 1 s check.
- `a_trade_count` / `b_trade_count` — counter channels.
- `a_depth` — scalar.
- `manual_quote` — human_driven channel, mean inter-quote 600 s, exponential draws via named substreams (cadence.go:27, :83-89).
- Diurnal seasonality on the wave state.

**Counter-as-hidden-state construction** (pinned, because `SimEvent.Seq` is ONE global run counter — schema [docs/contracts/sim-event-v0.1.schema.json:13](../../../docs/contracts/sim-event-v0.1.schema.json); [internal/world/observe.go:39,104](../../../internal/world/observe.go) — and no per-symbol counter exists in the envelope): trade counters are `threshold_integrator` states (direction above, threshold 0, gain 1) integrating the venue rate-driver state, so the emitted value is the time-integral rounded at read ([internal/world/reading_values.go:36-37](../../../internal/world/reading_values.go)). The domain declares counters are **activity integrals, not message counters** — per-event count truth lives exclusively in the delivery ledger's seq stream (`LedgerRecord.Seq`, [internal/model/event.go:38-49](../../../internal/model/event.go)).

### Dynamics and faults

Freeze construction: multiplicative delta −1 zeroing the rate driver (state_drivers.go:24-25) with threshold 0 keeps the integral pinned ([internal/world/integration_forms.go:97-102](../../../internal/world/integration_forms.go)). Band-deviation states (named, all data): `manual_quote` state is the band anchor; `band_dev_up` = saturation clamp [0, ∞] over (`a_mid` − `manual_quote` − `band_tolerance`); `band_dev_dn` = saturation over (`manual_quote` − tolerance − `a_mid`); `band_dev` = saturation over (up + dn) — two-sided |deviation| − tolerance, 0 inside the band. Constant states stay at initial ([internal/world/integration.go:108-111](../../../internal/world/integration.go)) and saturation is an instantaneous clamped pass-through (integration_forms.go:22-31), so no code is added.

Faults, detector form declared per fault:

| Fault | Mechanism (data) | Detector |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `feed_sequence_stall` | multiplicative −1 on a rate driver; the ONLY mechanism touching the counter stream — consumer-visible seq gaps come from drop/`delay_tail` perturbations alone, since counter resets are unmodelled | `single_channel_snr` on the frozen counter |
| `stale_quote` | venue-B mid pins to its clamp floor — a stuck-at print | `single_channel_snr` on `b_mid`; also arms the divergence detector |

### Perturbations — the graded envelope

Committed kinds only: `duplicate_burst`, `drop`, `reorder` (max_displacement 5), `delay_tail` (mean_s/sigma_s two-stage exponential, [internal/perturb/transform_timing.go:19-28](../../../internal/perturb/transform_timing.go)), `storm` (multiplier 3, [internal/perturb/transform_duplicate.go:31](../../../internal/perturb/transform_duplicate.go)).

**Allowance window (defined):** the domain-declared link-delay envelope (lognormal, link_delay.go:31-38) plus the declared `delay_tail` envelope; a record's lateness is `ObservedTimeNS − EventTimeNS` from its ledger row (event.go:44-45); inside/outside allowance is that comparison, not a new mechanism.

### Ground truth and its analytic oracle

- **Per-seq emission/delivery facts from the ledger** (exactly one primary row per emitted seq; duplicates, drops, delays as ledger rows) — counts-match-arrivals is the G9 trivial baseline.
- **Detector onsets from the noiseless dual-world solver.**
- **G2 boundary case:** halt commands are pinned to declared advance boundaries; records in flight across a halt keep arrival-based ledger attribution; every label cell is a pure function of the sealed inputs (domain digest, seed, command log) and replay reproduces it byte-for-byte.

### Effectors

- **`halt_symbol`** — interlocked on the two-sided band predicate `{state: band_dev, operator: lte, threshold: 0}`; the independent compliance system refuses a halt on a symbol NOT in a live breach — terminal refusal, no retry (the GROUND_TRUTH_AND_SCORING §3.1 interlock-handling cell, [docs/design/GROUND_TRUTH_AND_SCORING.md:197](../../design/GROUND_TRUTH_AND_SCORING.md)). The effect additively zeroes the venue-A rate driver so the activity counter freezes. `confirmation_channels: [a_trade_count]`; ack `failure_modes` include `silent_no_effect` — the ack says accepted and `a_trade_count` reports the **frozen counterfactual** (shadow state, [internal/world/state.go:57-63](../../../internal/world/state.go); effectors.go:159-169) while `b_trade_count` and both mids keep reporting truth: that asymmetry is the falsifiable false-success cell (§3.3 false-success rate, GROUND_TRUTH_AND_SCORING.md:231), strictly stronger than "counters keep moving". Idempotency window 300 s for exactly-once halt.
- **`suspend_smart_routing`** (advisory) — real throttling delta on a venue rate driver, no interlock: the approval-pressure handle at tempo.
