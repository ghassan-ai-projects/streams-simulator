# Code of Conduct

Streams Simulator is a collaborative open-source project. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and willing to revise claims when evidence changes.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and assume good faith while discussing evidence.
- Focus criticism on code, documentation, behavior, and decisions—not people.
- Make technical claims reproducible where practical.
- Respect privacy and do not publish secrets, credentials, or private test data.
- Help keep the project’s test-instrument trust model stronger than the systems it evaluates.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, malicious disruption, and knowingly misleading evidence are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, use the repository’s [GitHub security/contact page](https://github.com/ghassan-ai-projects/streams-simulator/security) to locate an available private channel. This repository does not currently publish a separate conduct email. Do not include secrets or unnecessary private data. Security vulnerabilities belong in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not a public issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and constructive.
